ABOUT

About Me

I am a theoretical condensed matter physicist interested in the electronic and optical properties of low-dimensional quantum materials.

My research combines first-principles electronic structure methods, many-body perturbation theory, excitonic models and numerical simulations to study light-matter interaction in two-dimensional and van der Waals materials.

I am particularly interested in excitons, carrier dynamics, nonlinear optical phenomena, terahertz physics and the connection between microscopic electronic structure and experimentally measurable optical spectra.

RESEARCH

Research Interests

01

Excitons

Excitonic states, bright-dark splitting, interlayer excitons and exciton dynamics in low-dimensional materials.

02

2D Materials

Electronic and optical properties of transition-metal dichalcogenides, hexagonal boron nitride and other van der Waals systems.

03

Ab Initio Methods

Density functional theory, GW quasiparticle calculations and Bethe-Salpeter equation calculations.

04

Nonlinear Optics

Nonlinear optical response, terahertz generation, difference-frequency generation and light-matter interactions.
