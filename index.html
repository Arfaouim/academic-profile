Excitons in 2D materials

Bound electron–hole excitations in monolayers, bilayers, twisted systems, and heterostructures, with emphasis on symmetry, valley physics, and screening.

Linear optical spectroscopy

Absorption, oscillator strength, polarization selection rules, band-resolved contributions, and excitonic fingerprints in optical spectra.

Linear optics Spectroscopy

Nonlinear optical response

Transport and dynamics
