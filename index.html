MA

About

My research focuses on the microscopic description of light-matter interaction in low-dimensional quantum materials. I am particularly interested in excitonic states, bright-dark exciton physics, carrier and valley dynamics, nonlinear optical response and ultrafast spectroscopy.

I use density functional theory, GW quasiparticle calculations, the Bethe-Salpeter equation, density-matrix approaches and effective theoretical models to connect electronic structure with optical observables.
